Four days in October. Mostly walking, eating and finding good light for photos.

![[lisbon.png|640]]

## Packing

- [x] Passport and tickets
- [x] Camera and a spare battery
- [ ] Comfortable shoes, the hills are steep
- [ ] Umbrella

## Days

1. Arrive, walk through Alfama, early dinner
2. Belém tower and the pastry shop
3. Day trip to Sintra
4. Slow morning, then the airport

> [!tip] Good to know
> Trams fill up fast. Walk uphill in the morning and ride down.
