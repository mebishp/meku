# Write like you think

## The slash menu

If you've used Notion, you already know this one. On an empty line, type `/` and a menu of things you can add appears: headings, lists, checklists, tables, quotes, code blocks.

Try it now. Click on the empty line below, type `/`, and pick **Heading 2**.



## Shortcuts you can type

Prefer to keep your hands on the keys? Start a line with these and Meku turns it into the right thing:

- `# ` then a space makes a big heading
- `## ` makes a smaller heading
- `- ` makes a bulleted list
- `1. ` makes a numbered list
- `> ` makes a quote

## Lists that nest

Press Enter in a list to get a new bullet. Press Enter on an empty bullet to end the list.

- Groceries
  - Fruit
    - Apples
    - Pears
  - Bread
- Things to read

Lists can go three levels deep.

## Changed your mind?

Press `Ctrl Z` to undo, as in any other app. Meku never changes your words behind your back, and every smart behaviour is one undo away.
