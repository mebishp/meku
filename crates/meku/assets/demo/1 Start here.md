# Welcome to Meku

Hi! This is a real notebook, and you are already inside it. Everything on this page is a note you can click into and change. Nothing here can break.

> [!note] Your first move
> Click anywhere in this paragraph and start typing. That's it, you're editing.

## How this works

Meku feels like a page in Notion: you just write. The one difference is that your notes are saved as ordinary files in a folder on your computer. No account, no sync, no waiting for anything to load.

Notice what happens when you click a line with a heading, like the one above. A few symbols appear at the start (`##`). Click somewhere else and they hide again. Those symbols are how Meku remembers that a line is a heading. You never have to type them if you don't want to.

## What is a mekuto?

This notebook is called a **mekuto**. It is a folder on your computer (here, `Meku` in your Documents) that holds your notes. You can keep as many mekutos as you like: one for work, one for your journal, one for a trip. In Notion terms, a mekuto is like a workspace.

Its name is at the top of the sidebar, with a small arrow. Click it any time to **switch** to another mekuto or create a new one.

When you're done, delete this whole folder if you like. It's only here to help you get started.
