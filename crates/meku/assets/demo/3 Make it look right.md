# Make it look right

## Bold, italic and friends

Wrap words in symbols and they change style. Click into the next line to see how it is written, then click away.

This is **bold**, this is *italic*, this is ~~crossed out~~, and this is `code`.

The symbols are the "markdown" you may have heard about. Think of it as typing a style instead of reaching for a toolbar. Nobody has to learn it all at once.

| You type | You get |
| --- | --- |
| `**word**` | **word** |
| `*word*` | *word* |
| `~~word~~` | ~~word~~ |
| `` `word` `` | `word` |

## Links

Write a link like this: `[Meku on GitHub](https://github.com/mebishp/meku)`. When the line isn't being edited it reads as just the words, and a click opens it.

## Quotes and callouts

> A quote starts with a `>` at the beginning of the line.

Callouts are the coloured boxes you may know from Notion. Use the `/` menu and pick **Callout**, or **Warning callout**:

> [!note] A note
> Good for tips and side remarks.

> [!warning] Heads up
> Good for things you don't want to miss.
